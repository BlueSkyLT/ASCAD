## Part C — Suggestions, by priority

### Evidence (what a reviewer will attack)

- **S1. The bottleneck claim is in the abstract but rests on one 50k run (likely one seed).** By the project's own rule (ledger: 50k = smoke scale) that cannot carry a headline claim.
  - Option (a): run variant A at 300k, two seeds. That is one Compact-sized training per seed; give a GPU-hour estimate before launching.
  - Option (b): move it out of the abstract and label it as a 50k observation.
- **S2. Uncertainty for "60 vs 75".**
  - The difference is three grid steps, with two seeds per model. Compact plain and both loss ablations are one seed.
  - Add bootstrap intervals over attack orderings (CPU only; posteriors are cached) and report seed ranges. This also settles whether "1.25× improvement" can stay.
- **S3. List the reproduction's deviations from MS23.**
  - The claim review notes MS23 trained without preprocessing and the released code uses 420k traces. This draft uses mean-trace subtraction, 400k minus 4 blanks, and a 5% validation split.
  - Say this in §4. The head-accuracy match supports "faithful"; hiding the deviations doesn't.
- **S4. GPAM fairness.**
  - The setup is one seed, one non-paper LR, a guessed optimizer, and not converged.
  - The raw-trace reproduction gives 600 vs the paper's 80. That 7.5× gap is the first thing a GPAM author will ask about. Explain it in the appendix (T-019 protocol) or drop the sentence.
  - A small LR sweep (3e-4 / 1e-3 / 3e-3) would turn "budget-matched" into a defensible comparison.
- **S5. Consider dropping w_C and L_dec from the CompactMultiResNet definition.**
  - Compact plain gives the same 60 / 70. The architecture-only story is simpler and removes the "is it the losses?" question.
  - L_dec is still needed in §7, where it is the object of study.
- **S6. Hand-typed numbers in the ladder.** The README rule says "tables are generated, never typed", but §7 has about 20 typed numbers.
  - I could not find a source for "80 traces (500 orderings)" for the fully labelled 300k model in the ledger. Confirm it.
  - Better: a generated ladder table (rung × seed × MTD1 / GE<1 / z / best-or-last).
- **S7. Test the "dilution" heuristic.** "0.0123 nats is consistent with it" can't be checked as written. Compute (1/16)·E[ℓ_own − 1] from the fully labelled model's C heads and put the predicted and measured margins side by side, or call it a heuristic and drop "consistent".

### Framing and related work

- **S8.** "The worst-case reference model itself has not been revisited" is too strong before the Scholar pass. GPAM and OvC both attack the worst case. Suggest: "its architecture has not been analysed".
- **S9. Small-model SCA is an established TCHES theme, and the paper doesn't position against it.**
  - Expected citations: the efficient-CNN methodology line (Zaid et al., TCHES 2020; Wouters et al., TCHES 2020) and RL-designed small networks (Rijsdijk et al., TCHES 2021). Verify the bib entries.
  - Without them a reviewer will say "small networks for SCA are known". The novelty is the *multi-task head* audit and the carrier mechanism, so say that explicitly.
- **S10. Two stories, one title.** The title and the first 6 sections are about parameters; §7 (the longest section) is about label requirements and has its own theory. Options:
  - (a) Keep both, but add a bridge sentence in the intro and one in §7: the carrier mechanism of §6 is what §7 trains without labels.
  - (b) Split §7 into a follow-up paper once the clean ladder cells (R4, α+C, R5) are filled. With 3 of 5 clean cells still open, (b) may produce two stronger papers.

### Metrics and notation

- **S11.** "GE=0" is really a success rate of 1 over 500 attacks; call it SR₅₀₀ = 1. "Full-key GE" usually means the rank of the 128-bit key (rank estimation); here it means the worst byte, so write "GE<1 on all bytes" consistently. "MTD" is often defined on a single attack, so define it at first use in tables too.
- **S12.** Define f_{π_j}(i|x) in words ("probability that slot j processes byte i") at Eq. (2). π is used for both the permutation and the head.

### Presentation

- **S13. Zero figures.** Add at least:
  - GE-vs-traces curves (Compact, MRN best and last, GPAM)
  - validation-loss curves showing MRN overfitting from epoch 4
  - an architecture sketch, MRN head vs Compact head
  - an oracle-swap bar chart (as-trained / uniform C / oracle C per model)
- **S14. Report compute.** Report training time and GPU memory, 137M vs 15.6M. That is a practical win the paper doesn't claim yet.
- **S15. Style.**
  - British (marginalise, initialisation, memorising) mixed with American (learned): pick one.
  - Thousands separators are inconsistent (1,145 vs 2250).
  - Table row labels are inconsistent: "MultiResNet (reprod.), best" lacks "s1"; "sub\_avg" is jargon (use "mean-trace sub."); the 16/16 column mixes "yes" with "0/16".
  - `\resizebox` makes table font sizes differ. Prefer `\small` + `tabular*`.
- **S16.** The abstract is ~230 words with five results. Consider cutting the GPAM sentence to half and moving the "factor of five" metric point to the intro.
